Subscribe to the link — don't download it. Downloading the file copies today's events in once and they never update. Subscribing keeps it live, so new leagues appear on their own.

How to add it

Google Calendar (do this on a computer)
  1. Open Google Calendar.
  2. In the left sidebar, next to Other calendars, click +.
  3. Choose From URL.
  4. Paste the address above and click Add calendar.

It then syncs to the Google Calendar app on your phone automatically. Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, so new events can take up to a day to appear.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open Settings and go to Apps → Calendar.
  2. Tap Accounts → Add Account → Other.
  3. Tap Add Subscribed Calendar.
  4. Paste the address above and tap Next, then Save.
Outlook
  1. Open Outlook Calendar.
  2. Click Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the address, give it a name, and click Import.

What's on it

  • Each league and tournament on the day it starts, with venue address and cost.
  • An all-day reminder on each signup deadline.
  • A map link, so tapping the location gives you directions.
Times are US Eastern (Indianapolis). Always confirm details on the organizer's own signup page before paying — this calendar is generated automatically and can lag behind changes.
